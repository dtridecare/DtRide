KYC status values for the admin panel

One status field per account, changed only from the admin panel. The app listens for changes and routes to the matching screen.

not_submitted -> pending -> approved
                          \-> rejected (with reason per item)

1. Start and sign in

Splash, onboarding, phone login with OTP, and driver account.

2. KYC approved by admin

Drivers upload documents. The admin approves or rejects each item with a reason.

3. Driving and earning

Requests with bidding or fixed fare, OTP trip start, subscription plans and earnings.

All screens are static design references for the Android build. Fares, plans and names are sample data.
